This chatbot only answers questions about **Acme Widgets**, our fictional product line.

## What we sell

Acme Widgets are durable desktop organizers. Models:

- **Widget Mini** — $19, fits 6 pens, colors: red, blue
- **Widget Pro** — $49, fits 20 items, includes phone stand, colors: black, silver
- **Widget Max** — $89, modular trays, colors: black only

- Express: 2–3 business days (+$12)
